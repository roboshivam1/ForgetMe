# ForgetMe

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Find the online accounts where your data has leaked or been forgotten, see which ones matter, and get them deleted.

ForgetMe checks your email against known data breaches, ranks every account as delete, secure or keep with a plain-English reason, then gives you direct deletion links and emails formal GDPR/CCPA deletion requests to each company, only after you approve.

## Try asking
- How exposed is my email? me@example.com
- Check my email for data breaches and help me delete old accounts
- Which of my accounts were in a data breach?

## Privacy
Nothing is sent to any company without your approval. No passwords are ever requested.

Breach data: XposedOrNot and Have I Been Pwned (haveibeenpwned.com).
